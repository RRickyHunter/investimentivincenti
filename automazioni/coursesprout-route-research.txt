## Enrol someone manually
1. \`coursesprout_list_pricing_options\` \u2192 the option covering what they should get.
2. \`coursesprout_get_member\` to check whether they already exist.
3. \`coursesprout_add_member\` with \`pricing_options: [id]\`. This creates a
   login and emails them.

## Change what an existing member can access
1. \`coursesprout_get_member\` \u2014 read their CURRENT access.
2. \`coursesprout_set_member_access\` with the FULL set you want them to end up
   with. Anything omitted is revoked. This is the single easiest way to
   accidentally take away something a member paid for.

## Run a discount
1. \`coursesprout_save_coupon\`.
2. \`coursesprout_get_order_form\` \u2192 order form id.
3. \`coursesprout_set_order_form_coupon\`.
4. Optionally \`coursesprout_save_scarcity_settings\` to add a countdown.

## Sell on instalments
1. \`coursesprout_save_payment_plan\` \u2014 type \`payment_plan\` with
   \`no_of_payment\`, or \`subscription\` for open-ended.
2. \`coursesprout_save_pricing_option\` with that \`payment_plan_id\`.

## Message members
1. \`coursesprout_get_announcement_audience\` \u2014 how many people this reaches.
2. \`coursesprout_preview_message\` \u2014 render the merge tags.
3. \`coursesprout_announce\` or \`coursesprout_broadcast_to_course\`.
Tell the user the audience size before sending.

## Destroys data with no undo
Every \`coursesprout_delete_*\` tool. The worst are:
- \`coursesprout_delete_all_members\` \u2014 empties the entire roster.
- \`coursesprout_delete_course\` \u2014 takes lessons, goals, pricing and order forms.
- \`coursesprout_delete_pricing_option\` \u2014 takes the order form and frees its slug.
